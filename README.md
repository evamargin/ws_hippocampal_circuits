# Hippocampal circuits: from substrate to dynamics and behaviour

Format: 15 weeks, 90 min, ≤12 Bsc/Msc students.

"Core" is the one review everybody in the club reads before the session. It is the paper the discussion is built around.
"Student-presented" is the second paper of the week. Only the presenting student reads it fully and gives a 10 to 15 minute summary, so the group gets the second perspective without doubling the reading load.
