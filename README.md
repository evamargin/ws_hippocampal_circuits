##  Proposed format for a small group

- **Before each class:**
	- The core review is split into sections, one per person. See `Sections distribution.md`.
	- The additional paper is optional. Eva might summarise it in class.
- **At home:**
	- Read the whole core review, your section with more care.
	- In `Sections distribution.md`: curiosity questions for everyone, and what to explain from your section. Prepare to teach it to the others: background, context, summary. Bring an explanation, not slides.
	- Questions about other sections are welcome.
- **In the class:**
	- Framing, 10 min: Eva gives the map and why this paper.
	- One slot per section: 8 min, the presenter explains in their own words from the paper, uninterrupted. Then 4 min of questions and discussion. Key ideas, key figures.
	- A timekeeper, rotating weekly, keeps the slots. We stop at 17:00. What is not covered opens the next class.
	- Eva's summary of the additional paper, 8 min.
	- Closing round, 10 min: one question from the curiosity guide, one sentence each.
	- **It s normal if we dont understand all details! We want to learn sth from these reviews, not everything :)**

## Proposed list

**Change of plan (November 2026).** We have spent enough time on circuits for now, so we switch to the LFP. First an introductory paper by Buzsáki that lays the ground, then the LFP patterns specific to the hippocampus. After the LFP come cell assemblies and sequences, which lead to manifolds. After manifolds and dynamics, we return to circuits to ground what we have learned. The last class has no new paper: we use it to consolidate what we read.
